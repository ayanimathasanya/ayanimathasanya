### 🧠 Explainable AI & Dengue Risk Analysis

As part of my final-year research, I am working on a **dengue outbreak prediction and explainability solution focused on the Colombo District**.

My research contribution focuses on making machine-learning results easier to understand by connecting model predictions with clear visual explanations.
